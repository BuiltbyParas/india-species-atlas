The three `*-habitat.webp` crops above are no longer drawn by the worlds (pass 2 replaced them with the cut-outs and the matted leopard); they are kept as the sources of those files. `snow-leopard-matted.webp` is no longer drawn either (the polish pass replaced it with `snow-leopard-cutout.webp`); it is kept as that file's source (its padding and the ledge's matte).

Each photograph except the rhinoceros's is the species record's own image (`src/assets/species/`), here at its larger size or uncropped; the study page credits it where it is shown.
